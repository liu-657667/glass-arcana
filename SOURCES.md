# 来源与权利记录

- A. E. Waite, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot*, first published 1910. Transcription reference: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Pictorial_Key_to_the_Tarot . Parts II/III are the intended symbol / divinatory reference. We have not supplied a verified translation or copied passages in this build.
- Pamela Colman Smith, early Rider–Waite–Smith illustrations: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck . Review each file's date, edition and license before including art. Early original art and modern recolorings are not interchangeable rights-wise.
- All Chinese themes, reflection prompts and concise study descriptions in `src/cards.ts` are original project-written drafts, **not quotations or certified historical meanings**. `source` records intended verification source, not completed verification.
- No third-party card images or third-party modern interpretation datasets are bundled. CSS ornaments are interface placeholders, not final card artwork.
- Vue, Vite, TypeScript and tooling retain their own package licenses. A lockfile captures versions.

Review checklist for the next edition: verify all 78 identities; compare every observable symbol with specified early image; separately cite historical symbolism/divination and modern interpretation; record exact section/file URL and verification date; review translations; audit suits/number constraints; verify artist attribution and each asset's permission. This work is not complete yet.
