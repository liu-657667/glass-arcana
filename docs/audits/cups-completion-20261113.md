# Cups: remaining twelve originals, scoped count and motif review

Date: 2026-11-13. Base: `82c2aa8d32cc7f2e9a6a51c6f1c61ee0c354736e`.

## Scope and method

This pass viewed the actual original PNG pixels for Cups Ace–Six, Nine–Ten and Page–King (12 cards), individually at 1024×1536, and viewed the corresponding historical reference JPEGs. Cups Seven and Eight are **not re-inspected in this pass**; their earlier scoped review is in [cups-seven-eight-20261113.md](cups-seven-eight-20261113.md). Taken together these documents cover basic count/composition for all 14 Cups, not exhaustive symbol certification.

References: A. E. Waite / Pamela Colman Smith, *The Pictorial Key to the Tarot*, historical [1911] illustrations hosted by Sacred Texts, `https://scrape.sacred-texts.com/tarot/pkt/img/cu{code}.jpg`. Codes are listed below. These are temporary study references, not copied into this repository. The site's US public-domain context is not a worldwide rights clearance. Modern recolorings were not used. Small reference pixels limit fine anatomy and micro-symbol comparisons.

Counts below count actual chalices, not floral decorations or architectural lobes. Court rank numbers 11–14 do **not** mean 11–14 cups. No artwork, derivative, manifest, application code, final-status label, release or deployment was changed. Count agreement alone is not final acceptance.

## Outcome and remaining gates

All 12 inspected originals have the expected main cup count. This is a limited count/composition result, **not** a verdict that all symbols match or that artwork is release-final. The especially useful correction candidates are Ace's missing scattered drops, Six's guard/support motif, Knight's fish/boot-wing motifs, and Queen's strongly simplified vessel/throne. Whether retaining any stylized difference is acceptable depends on the project's fidelity target; this pass does not silently label them fixed.

Remaining: the other 42 minor cards were not visually inspected in this pass; earlier Sword Ace and Pentacles Nine findings remain in their own report. Full-resolution hands/faces, exact botanical/species identification, minor engraved symbols, and all responsive derivatives were not exhaustively certified. Browser behavior, Safari, mobile, AI and licenses are separate gates, not inferred from these images. No test suite was rerun for this documentation-only addition; `git diff --check` is the applicable document validation.
